AnyLogic for everyone: everyday-sim

Turn a question from daily life or a small business into a simulation you can play and adjust.

You don't need to know modeling. Tell the agent your question, such as "how many staff at lunch?", "how long to save a down payment?" or "how fast will word of mouth spread?". It picks a modeling method, asks you for at most 3 key numbers, and builds a page like this one:

  • Animation: watch how things unfold, for example when the line starts to build.
  • Results: the key numbers plus the range you'd see in most runs, because real life involves luck.
  • What if…: drag a slider, the model re-runs right away and is compared with the default.
  • Model: what the model assumes and what it leaves out, so you can judge how far to trust it.

Left out on purpose

  • No drag-and-drop modeling canvas. Most people can't draw a correct model, so you describe it in conversation and the agent writes the model file.
  • No 3D or maps. They cost a lot and help little with answering the question.
  • No claim of exact prediction. The goal is to compare options and see trends, so every page states its assumptions and limits.

Use and download

Download the everyday-sim skill (ZIP) and unpack it for an AI agent that supports skills. Building pages requires Python 3.8+ and Node 16+, with no extra packages.

These numbers are illustrative assumptions, not forecasts. Change the inputs in What if, and review the model assumptions and limitations.
